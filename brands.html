How it works

  1. The question. We pick one your customers really ask, together with you: "heat pump at −15 °C?", "does one degree matter?", "charge at night?".
  2. The simulation. We model it in Python, minute by minute, with stated assumptions and real prices. You get the numbers file.
  3. The video. Animated, voiced and captioned, with a loop and a clear end card. One round of changes.
Our one rule: the simulation decides the answer. We only make a video when the honest result is good for your product. That is why people share these videos, and why they trust them.
