Privacy

Code Plays Itself · last updated 14 November 2026

The calculator on this site

The heating calculator runs entirely in your browser. What you type in is never sent anywhere, and this site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub's privacy statement).

Our YouTube uploader

"Code Plays Itself uploader" is a small tool that only we use, to upload and schedule videos on our own YouTube channel through the YouTube API Services. It signs in to our own channel only. It does not collect, store or share data about anyone else. The sign-in key stays on our own computer.

Using it means agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google handles data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Messages

If you comment a keyword such as CALC on Instagram, a small bot replies with a link in a direct message. To avoid messaging you twice, it keeps the comment's ID, your Instagram account ID and your username in a log on our own computer. That record is never shared or sold, and we delete it on request (send us a DM on Instagram).
